# Shop Book privacy notice

Version 1

Shop Book helps a business keep its own records: products, stock, sales, customers and what they owe, expenses, and the team who records them. This notice explains what Shop Book keeps, why, and what control you have.

## What Shop Book keeps

- **About you and your team:** names, phone numbers, email addresses (if given), your role in the business, your language, and when each phone last connected. Passwords are never kept: only a scrambled form of them (a hash) that can't be turned back into the password.
- **Your business's records:** everything you and your staff enter, such as products and prices, stock, sales, payments, debts, expenses and day closes, with who recorded each one and when.
- **Your customers:** the names, phone numbers and amounts owed that you enter.
- **Technical details:** for example error reports and the time of each request, so problems can be found and fixed. These never include your customers' details, your passwords or the contents of your records.

## Why

Only to run Shop Book for your business: to keep your records, show them to the people you allow, sync them between your team's phones, and send account emails (confirming your email, resetting a password). Your records are **never sold** and **never used for advertising**.

## Who can see your records

- **Only your business.** No other business, including your neighbours, can see your products, prices, customers or sales.
- **Inside your business,** each person sees what their role allows. Only the owner sees cost prices and profit.
- **The developer** may look at technical details to fix a problem, and helps only when you ask (for example to reset an owner's password after confirming who you are).
- Your records are stored with hosting companies (for the server, the database and email) that keep them on Shop Book's behalf.

## Your customers' details

You decide which customers to add. Use their details only for your own records and the messages you send them yourself. Shop Book doesn't contact your customers.

## Your choices

- **Download your records** at any time (the owner, as CSV files).
- **Close your business:** staff access ends at once, and every record is permanently deleted 30 days later. You can cancel within those 30 days.
- **Change your details** (phone number, email, password) in Settings.

Ghana's Data Protection Act applies to the personal details kept in Shop Book.

## Questions

Use **Help → Report a problem** in the app.
