# Shop Book terms of use

Version 1

By using Shop Book you agree to these terms.

## Your account

- Keep your password to yourself. Each person in your team uses their own account and their own phone.
- The owner adds staff, chooses their roles, and can remove them at any time.
- You're responsible for what is recorded with your account.

## Your records

- Your business's records belong to your business.
- Confirmed sales, payments, repayments, stock entries, expenses and day closes **can't be changed or deleted**; mistakes are fixed with a correction that shows beside the original. This keeps the records trustworthy.
- Shop Book works out totals, balances and stock from what is recorded. Check important figures against your cash and stock, especially when a phone has been offline.

## Fair use

- Use Shop Book only for your business's lawful records.
- Don't try to reach another business's records, or to disrupt the service.
- Enter customers' details only for your own records and messages, and with care.

## The service

- Shop Book is being tested with a small number of shops. It may sometimes be slow or unavailable, and it may change.
- The app keeps working without internet and sends your records when it can.
- Download your records regularly as your own copy.

## Closing your business

The owner can close the business in the app. Staff access ends at once, and all records are permanently deleted after 30 days, unless the owner cancels before then.

## Changes to these terms

If these terms change, the app will ask you to accept the new version.
